## 1. Bottom line

Your budget is comfortable. At €1,000pp you have **~$11,500 for 10 people** (~$958/night all-in). Multiple options land at half that. The binding constraint is not money — it is that **downtown San Diego has almost no 4-bedroom whole homes**, because downtown is condo towers.

Three findings shape everything below:

1. **Nothing 4BR+ is genuinely walking distance to the Hyatt.** The closest whole-home options are Gaslamp lofts with *one bathroom for ten people*. Realistic choices are Little Italy (2 trolley stops) or Bankers Hill (7 min drive).
2. **The trolley solves this.** County Center/Little Italy and Seaport Village are both on the Green Line, ~2 stops apart, and Seaport Village station (530 W Market St) is at the Hyatt's doorstep. Treat Little Italy as effectively adjacent.
3. **Coronado is legally impossible** — 26-night minimum, city-wide. Rule it out entirely.

## 3. Hard constraints

**Coronado — eliminated.** Coronado Municipal Code ch. 86.78 bans transient rentals under **26 consecutive nights**, city-wide, including guesthouses and ADUs. Confirmed independently by two agents, including a Vrbo listing stating it in its own text. Any 12-night Coronado booking is non-compliant.

**Occupancy caps may force 5 bedrooms.** San Diego's reported rule is **2 guests per bedroom + 2**, ceiling 14 overnight. That caps a 4BR at **10 people** and a 5BR at 12 — regardless of how many beds fit. *Sourced from secondary references, not confirmed in the Municipal Code — verify before booking if your group is 11–12.* This is the most likely way a large group trips enforcement.

**STRO licensing.** Stays under one month require a City of San Diego STRO licence — Tier 3 for a whole home. Downtown supply is capped and scarce: ZIP 92101 holds just **448 of 7,954 citywide licences (5.6%)**, versus 30% in Mission/Pacific Beach. Every downtown Airbnb listing checked displayed a valid `STR-#####L` number, so enforcement has cleaned up the platform. **Still verify the number against the City's [STRO OpenData portal](https://opendatasandiego.com/short-term-residential-occupancy)** — check the tier is 3 and the expiry falls after 7 Dec 2026.

**HOA rules are a separate gate.** Roughly 40–60% of San Diego condos carry CC&R short-term limits, and Marina/downtown high-rises skew restrictive — many impose 30-day minimums. A city licence does not override the HOA. For any downtown condo, **get written confirmation the HOA permits stays under 30 nights.**

**Tax.** San Diego moved to zone-based TOT under Measure C (May 2025): **11.75% / 12.75% / 13.75%**, highest near the Convention Center. Observed all-in uplift ran a consistent 11.7–13.95% across platforms. Rule of thumb: **advertised nightly × 12, then add ~12.5%.**

## 5. Booking tactics

1. **Check both platforms for any property you shortlist.** The same homes appear on Airbnb and Vrbo at different prices, and neither wins consistently — 2330 Historic was cheaper on Airbnb, the 5BR Little Italy cheaper on Vrbo.
2. **Use Travelocity, not Expedia.** Identical Expedia Group inventory and backend, but Travelocity quoted **0.6–1.8% cheaper** on the same properties.
3. **Book direct where you can.** No platform service fee is worth **~13% more house** for the same spend — the difference between a 4BR and a 5BR.
4. **Sign up for Kasa's free account** if you go that route — it's an ~11% discount ($1,010 on the bundle). Book the 2×2BR as a *bundle*, not two reservations: saves $274 because the resort fee is charged once.
5. **Filter for bathrooms, not just bedrooms.** Add `&min_bathrooms=3` to Airbnb searches. Over 12 nights with 10 adults, the 1-bath Gaslamp lofts are a trip-ruiner regardless of price or location.
6. **Ask about included parking spaces.** Two rental cars in a downtown garage for 12 nights runs **~$720** — 6.5% of your budget. Kasa Little Italy charges $50/night. Bankers Hill and Point Loma houses typically include driveway parking free.
7. **Move soon on the cheap, well-reviewed units.** Only 2 Kasa bundles remained at time of check, and these are dynamic-pricing properties 16 months out.

## 6. Logistics

**Getting around.** Seaport Village station sits at the Hyatt; the Green Line reaches Little Italy in ~2 stops and Gaslamp Quarter station is opposite the Convention Center. Fares via the PRONTO app. The trolley does *not* usefully reach Mission Beach or La Jolla. **Airport (SAN) is 6 miles / 5–15 minutes, ~$14–24** — a non-issue.

**Weather.** Late Nov highs ~21°C, lows ~13°C; **November is San Diego's sunniest month**. Early Dec cools to ~19°C/11°C and December is the wettest month. Sunset is ~17:00 — plan outdoor activity for mornings. Beach weather for walking, not swimming.

**Thanksgiving is day 2 of your stay (Thu 26 Nov).** Most shops and many restaurants closed; supermarkets close early. **Do a full grocery run on arrival day** — a strong argument for a place with a real kitchen. Book any Thanksgiving restaurant meal now. Black Friday (27th) means heavy traffic and packed parking.

**December Nights, Balboa Park** — California's largest free holiday festival, 350,000+ people over two days, free museum entry in the evenings. Falls near the end of your stay; take the trolley, parking will be impossible. *Sources disagreed on exact 2026 dates — confirm at [sandiego.gov/december-nights](https://www.sandiego.gov/december-nights).*

**Also worth checking:** the San Diego Bay Parade of Lights, a December boat parade visible from the Embarcadero — practically from the Hyatt's doorstep. 2026 dates not verified.

## 7. What is not verified

- **Exact addresses and walk times.** Platforms withhold addresses pre-booking. Every distance here is estimated from neighbourhood centroids, not routed.
- **Location of items #6 (Luxury 5BR Pool Home) and the Casa Paradiso / waterslide properties.** These surfaced via loose radius searches; their slugs suggest suburban lots. **Check the map pin before assuming walkability.**
- **Airbnb "est." totals** are base price plus calculated tax — Airbnb renders its fee breakdown client-side and it could not be read. Travelocity/Kasa figures are genuine quoted all-in.
- **Occupancy limits (2/bedroom + 2, max 14).** Secondary sources only, not confirmed in the Municipal Code.
- **STRO licence validity.** Numbers were confirmed as *displayed*; none were cross-checked against the City register.
- **All chain-hotel rates.** Hilton, Hyatt, IHG, Marriott and Booking.com all blocked automated access. No rate was invented for any of them.
- **Coverage.** ~13 of 426 available downtown properties were sampled on Expedia Group, plus ~60 on Airbnb. Better inventory almost certainly exists — the search URLs above will show everything in a browser.
- **Prices are today's quotes**, 16 months out, on dynamic-pricing properties. Expect drift.
